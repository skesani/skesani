## 💛 The cause

Daycare waitlists fill months out. A closed school day still lands on a parent’s calendar. Plenty of sitters live on the same street and want fair, flexible work — and families still cannot meet them with proof they can trust.

**That gap is why I built [Genzy Nanny](https://genzynanny.com).**

Not another classifieds board. A system where identity, availability, booking, chat, and pay stay in one place — so parents can look someone up before they knock, and sitters get booked by families who already trust the process.

> Help is often next door. Trust should be too.

## 🍼 Genzy Nanny — childcare, reimagined

**[Genzy Nanny](https://genzynanny.com)** is a USA childcare marketplace I founded and built end to end: web, iOS, backend, payments, identity, and ops.

Parents find verified sitters nearby. Sitters set rates and get paid in the app. Instant Booking alerts eligible caregivers in waves until the first accept wins.

### What it does

| For parents | For sitters |
| --- | --- |
| Browse nearby sitters with ratings, reviews, and trust badges | Professional profile, portfolio photos, and intro video |
| Instant Booking — one request, nearby sitters alerted, first accept wins | Set your own rate, hours, and Instant Booking preference |
| Scheduled care for pickups, date nights, after school, overnights | Accept, decline, or bid — you control the calendar |
| Stripe checkout with the total visible before confirm | Direct payouts after the session; sitters keep **95%** of care earnings |
| In-app chat only after a real relationship | Reviews and verification badges that travel with you |
| Save trusted sitters to your flock | Language-aware discovery (including Telugu, Tamil, Hindi, Arabic) |

### Trust before the first hello

- Government ID + facial match (Didit)
- Background check review
- CPR / First Aid badges
- Public names as first name + last initial until a booking exists
- Secure in-app messaging and Stripe-backed escrow-style payments

### How Instant Booking works

1. Parent posts time, place, kids, and budget  
2. Nearby opted-in sitters are ranked by proximity and trust  
3. Alerts go out in stages (immediate, then expanding waves)  
4. First successful accept gets the job  
5. Card authorizes up front and captures only when a sitter is confirmed
